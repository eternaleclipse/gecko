# Themes

[Docs](README.md)

Pick one with **Change Theme** in the palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>). Themes preview live as you move through the list. The choice is saved in `~/.gecko-terminal/settings.json` and follows you to every window and device. The desktop app can also make any theme see-through (**Background opacity…** in the palette).

## Gecko

The default. **Gecko** follows the system's light or dark mode; **Gecko Dark** and **Gecko Light** pin one.

## Designed

Themes that change more than colors.

### Digital Watch

The window becomes a resin wristwatch whose LCD is the terminal. It resizes with the window, has a ticking 7-segment face, and the LIGHT, MODE, START·STOP and ALARM pushers work.

<img src="img/themes/digital-watch.jpg" alt="Digital Watch theme">

### Mission Control

A launch-webcast skin with DIN-style type.
